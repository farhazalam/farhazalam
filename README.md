### Hi, I'm Farhaz.

I'm a developer based in Dubai. I build mobile apps, websites and the backend connections that make them work. My experience includes freelance projects, nearly five years at Tata Consultancy Services, and my current role at Beyond Limits Cloud Services.

I like the parts of development that become visible in everyday use: a booking that takes fewer steps, an app that still works offline, or a trip that gets recorded without someone remembering to press a button.

### What I've been building

- **Background trip tracking** for a mileage app, with rules to detect trip starts and ends, and polylines to show the route travelled.
- **A knowledge-base AI chatbot**, integrated into a mobile app.
- **An AI research system** that uses browser automation.
